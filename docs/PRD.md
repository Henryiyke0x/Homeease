# HomeEase — Product Requirements Document

## Vision
A managed home-services platform connecting customers with trusted professionals for household tasks.

## User groups
- Customers: discover services, book, pay, track and review.
- Professionals: apply, verify identity, manage availability and jobs.
- Administrators: manage users, services, bookings, assignments, payments and disputes.

## MVP
1. Public marketing website and service catalogue.
2. Customer registration and login.
3. Guided service booking and address management.
4. Customer booking history and status.
5. Professional application and job dashboard.
6. Admin management of services, users and bookings.
7. Payment integration (Paystack planned).
8. Booking notifications.

## Later enhancements
Real-time updates, subscriptions, smart matching, reviews, disputes, analytics, referrals and AI booking assistance.

## Booking lifecycle
PENDING → CONFIRMED → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED. A booking may also be CANCELLED under platform policy.
